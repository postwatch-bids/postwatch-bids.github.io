About & FAQ

What is PostWatch?

A free, independent list of open security guard, armed/unarmed guard and patrol services opportunities. We pull from public sources (SAM.gov Contract Opportunities, state procurement portals) and recent awards from USAspending.gov.

How do I bid on a federal guard contract?

Open the notice on SAM.gov, download the solicitation and attachments, check the set-aside and required licenses (state guard licensing, armed qualifications, insurance), attend any site visit, submit questions by the Q&A deadline, then submit your proposal exactly as the instructions say before the response deadline.

How do I register in SAM.gov?

Create a Login.gov account, then register your entity at SAM.gov to get a Unique Entity ID (UEI). Have your EIN, bank info and NAICS codes (561612 Security Guards and Patrol Services) ready. Registration is free and must be renewed every year. It usually takes 1-4 weeks to become active.

What do the notice types mean?

Sources Sought: market research, so respond to get on the radar. Presolicitation: a bid is coming. Solicitation / Combined Synopsis: open for proposals now.

What is a set-aside?

Contracts reserved for certain businesses, for example Small Business, 8(a), SDVOSB, WOSB or HUBZone. Your certifications determine which ones you can bid on.

Data comes from public sources: SAM.gov, USAspending.gov, the Texas ESBD, LA County, City of LA (data.lacity.org) and public OpenGov portals. It may be incomplete or out of date, so always verify details on the original notice before you bid. PostWatch is not affiliated with any government agency.
